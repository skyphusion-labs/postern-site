Privacy

This site does not track you

Privacy is the main goal of Postern, and this site follows the same rule.

This website

  • No cookies. The site sets none.
  • No trackers and no analytics. There is no analytics script and no tracking pixel.
  • Nothing from anyone else. Every script, stylesheet, image and font comes from this site. There are no third-party fonts, embeds or frames. The site's Content Security Policy enforces this in your browser, and its CI fails any change that adds one.
  • Your theme choice stays in your browser. If you pick light or dark, the choice is saved in this browser's local storage. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Hosting. Cloudflare serves the site. Like any web host, it receives each request, including your IP address and browser details, in order to answer it. We have not turned on Worker logs or analytics for this site.

The site's source is public, so you can check every claim above: skyphusion-labs/postern-site.

The Postern software

Postern is software you run yourself, on your own Cloudflare account. Skyphusion Labs operates no mail service, so we hold none of your mail. The public demo mailbox is the one instance we run, and anything sent to it is public.
